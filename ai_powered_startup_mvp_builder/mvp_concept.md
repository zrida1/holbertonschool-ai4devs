# MVP Concept – TaskMaster

## 1. Problem Statement

Students and small teams often struggle to organize their tasks, manage deadlines, and track project progress. Using multiple tools or managing tasks manually can make it difficult to stay organized and prioritize important work.

TaskMaster is a simple AI-powered task management application that helps users organize their work, break down complex tasks, and monitor their progress in one place.

## 2. Target Users

- University students managing assignments and projects.
- Freelancers organizing their daily work.
- Small teams collaborating on projects.
- Individuals who want to improve their productivity and time management.

## 3. Core Features

### 1. Task Creation and Management
Users can create, edit, delete, and view tasks. Each task includes a title, description, priority, status, and deadline.

### 2. AI Task Breakdown
Users can enter a complex task, and the AI assistant suggests smaller, manageable subtasks to help them organize their work.

### 3. Task Prioritization
Users can assign priorities such as Low, Medium, and High to their tasks.

### 4. Progress Dashboard
A dashboard displays the total number of tasks, completed tasks, pending tasks, and overdue tasks.

### 5. Task Filtering and Search
Users can search for tasks and filter them by status, priority, or deadline.

## 4. Constraints

- The MVP will focus on individual users rather than large team collaboration.
- User authentication may be excluded from the initial version.
- The application will initially support a single language.
- AI task breakdown will depend on an available AI API.
- The application will use a limited set of task statuses: To Do, In Progress, and Completed.
- The initial version will focus on essential functionality rather than advanced project management features.
- The application will be designed for desktop and mobile browsers.
