# Product Idea: Carpool Connect

## 1. Vision Statement

Carpool Connect is a corporate commute management platform designed to connect employees who travel to similar destinations. Its goal is to make daily commuting more affordable, convenient, and environmentally friendly by encouraging employees to share rides.

The platform aims to reduce commuting costs, traffic congestion, and carbon emissions while improving the daily travel experience for employees.

## 2. Primary Users and Their Needs

### Drivers
- Want to share empty seats in their cars with other employees.
- Want to reduce fuel and transportation costs.
- Need an easy way to find passengers travelling in the same direction.

### Passengers
- Want affordable and reliable transportation to work.
- Need to find available rides based on their location and schedule.
- Want to coordinate trips with trusted colleagues.

### Company Administrators
- Want to manage and monitor employee commuting activities.
- Need tools to encourage ride-sharing among employees.
- Want to reduce the company's environmental impact.

## 3. Key Features

- **Ride Matching:** Match drivers and passengers based on their routes, locations, and schedules.
- **Ride Scheduling:** Allow users to create and schedule regular or one-time trips.
- **User Profiles:** Display employee information and ride preferences.
- **Booking System:** Allow passengers to request available seats.
- **Ratings and Reviews:** Enable users to rate their ride-sharing experiences.
- **Notifications:** Notify users about booking requests, confirmations, and trip updates.
- **Eco-Impact Tracking:** Show estimated fuel savings and carbon emission reductions.

## 4. Differentiators

- **Corporate Focus:** Designed specifically for employees within organizations.
- **Verified Users:** Restricts access to authorized company employees to build trust.
- **Smart Ride Matching:** Matches users based on commuting routes and schedules.
- **Environmental Awareness:** Encourages sustainable transportation through eco-impact tracking.
- **Cost Efficiency:** Helps employees reduce daily commuting expenses.
