## Chosen Problem Statement

Network problems can be difficult to understand and troubleshoot, especially for users with limited networking knowledge. Users often do not know what causes connection issues or which steps they should take to solve them.

Our solution is an **AI Network Troubleshooting Assistant** that analyzes user-reported network problems, suggests possible causes, and provides simple step-by-step troubleshooting instructions.

## MVP Features

1. **Problem Input** – Users can describe their network issues, such as no internet connection, slow internet, or DNS errors.
2. **AI-Powered Analysis** – AI analyzes the reported problem and identifies possible causes.
3. **Troubleshooting Steps** – The application provides practical instructions and relevant network commands to help users investigate the issue.
4. **Command Explanations** – Users can learn what commands such as `ping`, `ipconfig`, and `nslookup` do.
5. **Result History** – Users can view their previous troubleshooting requests and recommendations.
